# Onboarding at Northwind Robotics

New engineers are issued a laptop on their first morning. The laptop comes from the IT
desk on the second floor, and you will need your signed offer letter to collect it.

Accounts are created the day before you start. If you cannot log in, the account was
probably created against a personal email by mistake; contact the IT desk rather than
creating a second account, because duplicate accounts cause payroll problems later.

Your first week is deliberately light. You are expected to read the engineering
handbook, set up your development environment, and ship one small change to production
by Friday. Shipping something in week one is a tradition rather than a test, and nobody
has ever been judged on how small the change was.

Every new engineer is assigned a buddy for their first month. The buddy is not your
manager and is not responsible for your work; they exist so there is somebody obvious to
ask an obvious question. Buddies are chosen from a different team to your own, on
purpose, so you meet somebody outside your immediate group.
