# Expense policy

Expenses under two hundred pounds do not need pre-approval. Anything above that must be
approved by your manager before you spend the money, not afterwards.

Travel is booked through the company travel portal. Booking outside the portal is
allowed only when the portal has no suitable option, and you must note the reason on
the claim. Trains are preferred to flights for journeys under four hours.

Claims are submitted monthly and must be in by the fifth of the following month.
Claims submitted after the fifth are paid in the next cycle, which means waiting an
extra month. Receipts are required for everything over ten pounds.

Client entertainment has its own limit of eighty pounds per head, and requires the name
of the client on the claim. Alcohol is reimbursable with a meal and not otherwise.

Home office equipment is a separate budget of five hundred pounds per engineer per year,
and does not count against the normal expense limits.
