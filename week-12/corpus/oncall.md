# On-call

The on-call rotation is one week long and runs from Wednesday morning to Wednesday
morning. Wednesday was chosen so that a handover never lands on a Monday.

Being on call means carrying the pager and being reachable within fifteen minutes. It
does not mean working evenings as a matter of course; most weeks the pager does not go
off at all.

If you are paged, the first thing to do is acknowledge it. Acknowledging stops the
escalation timer. Fixing can wait five minutes; acknowledging cannot.

Escalation goes to the secondary after ten minutes without acknowledgement, and to the
engineering manager after twenty. Nobody has ever been criticised for escalating early.

Every page produces a short write-up within two working days, whether or not anything
was broken. The write-up is not a blame exercise and does not require a root cause; a
page that turned out to be noise is still worth recording, because repeated noise is
how alerts get fixed.

Time spent on call out of hours is compensated as time off in lieu, at the rate of one
day per disturbed night.
